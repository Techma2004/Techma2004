About

A bit about how I got here.

I've always learned best by finishing things not tutorials, not toy examples, complete systems that real people rely on. That habit is how a school in Calabar ended up running its student records on software I built myself, and it's why my current project isn't a chatbot demo but something closer to a full operating layer.

Working from Nigeria has shaped the way I build more than any framework choice has. Power isn't always reliable and bandwidth isn't always cheap, so I design for offline first, local execution by default not as a limitation to work around, but as a discipline that tends to produce better, leaner software anyway.

I recently graduated and now work independently, which means I own every part of what I build the architecture decisions, the late debugging sessions, and the parts that are unglamorous but have to be right. I'm currently deepening my security background alongside my regular project work, because I want to understand the systems I build well enough to know how they break.
